TV Internal Parts Course

This course covers the internal components of a TV and how they work together to deliver high-quality visuals and sound.

What You'll Learn:

Topic Description
Screen Technology LCD, LED, OLED, and QLED displays.
Motherboard The brain of the TV, controlling all functions.
Power Supply How power is distributed to all components.
Tuner and Connectivity How the TV receives signals from cable, satellite, or streaming devices.

Course Duration:

4 weeks (2 hours per session, 2 sessions per week)
